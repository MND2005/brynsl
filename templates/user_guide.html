BRYN User Guide

1 Create Account

Create account to access all features and services from the sign up page.

2 Verify Your Email

After signing up, BRYN will send a verification OTP to your email. Enter the OTP and verify your email.

3 Log In

After verifying your email, login to your account using your email and password.

4 Chat With BRYN

After logging in, you can start chatting with BRYN.

5 Additional Guides

When You Forgot Your Password

1 Go to login page and click forgot password.

2 Enter your registered email here.

3 BRYN will send you an OTP code via email. Check your email and enter the OTP. After enter your new password and click reset password button.

4 After that you will be redirected automatically to Login page. Now you can Login with your new password.

How To Make A Payment

1 After 7 days free trial you will automatically be redirected to payment required page.

2 You can make a payment through CDM machine or online transaction according to provided details in payment required page and send the payment receipt to provided numbers via WhatsApp with your registered email or mobile number.
